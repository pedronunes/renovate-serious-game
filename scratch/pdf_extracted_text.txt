atomisation and spray distribution, while

flow rate using an appropriate nozzle
size and a suitable operating pressure .

NON-UNIFORM CANOPY
When the canopy is not uniform, the
total flow rate can be distributed
according to the vegetation density.
Different nozzle sizes may be used in
each section of the sprayer, but the
nozzle configuration should be
symmetrical on both sides of the
sprayer, and all nozzles must operate at
the same pressure.
